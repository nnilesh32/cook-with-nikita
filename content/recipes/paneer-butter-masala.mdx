This is the curry that makes people who claim they don't like paneer change their minds. The whole trick is the cashew paste — it's what gives the gravy its body without needing more cream than is reasonable.

## Method

1. Simmer the chopped tomatoes and cashews in a cup of water for 10 minutes, until the tomatoes are fully soft. Cool slightly and blend to a smooth paste.
2. Melt the butter in a pan. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook until fragrant, about a minute.
3. Pour in the tomato-cashew paste and cook on medium heat for 8–10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it thickens and the raw tomato smell is gone.
4. Add the chilli powder, sugar and salt. Cook for another 2 minutes.
5. Add the paneer cubes and simmer gently for 5 minutes — don't boil hard, or the paneer turns rubbery.
6. Stir in the cream, garam masala and crushed kasuri methi. Simmer for a final 2 minutes.

Finish with a small knob of butter on top and serve with naan or jeera rice.
